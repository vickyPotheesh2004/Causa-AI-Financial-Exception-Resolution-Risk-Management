# 🚀 Final Project

# **AI Financial Exception Resolution & Risk Management System**

### One-line definition

> **An evidence-driven AI financial operations system that detects, investigates, evaluates, risk-assesses, resolves, rejects, or escalates financial exceptions across payment, settlement, refund, fee, adjustment, and bank records — while enforcing applicable rules and permissions, creating department tickets and reminders for unresolved cases, and maintaining a complete searchable, source-backed audit trail from detection to final resolution.**

---

# 1. The Problem

Financial operations can involve multiple connected records:

```text
Payment
   ↓
Order
   ↓
Refund
   ↓
Fees
   ↓
Adjustments
   ↓
Settlement
   ↓
Bank Record
```

An exception can occur anywhere in this lifecycle.

For example:

```text
Payment = ₹10,000
Expected Settlement = ₹9,750
Actual Settlement = ₹9,500
                         ↓
                   ₹250 mismatch
```

A finance employee then has to manually:

- find the transaction,
- find the settlement,
- inspect fees,
- inspect refunds,
- compare bank records,
- determine what happened,
- check applicable rules,
- determine whether the issue is valid,
- assess financial/fraud/compliance risk,
- decide whether it can be resolved,
- contact the appropriate department,
- track the ticket,
- remember to follow up,
- and finally document everything.

The problem is therefore **not simply reconciliation**.

The deeper problem is:

> **When financial records do not agree, there is no unified evidence-driven workflow that can investigate the exception, determine what is actually known, assess its risk, apply the appropriate rules and authority boundaries, resolve safe cases, escalate uncertain or fraud-related cases, and track the issue until closure.**

---

# 2. What the system actually solves

The system converts:

```text
"Something doesn't match."
```

into:

```text
"What happened?"
       ↓
"What evidence proves it?"
       ↓
"Which rules apply?"
       ↓
"Who has authority to decide?"
       ↓
"What is the risk?"
       ↓
"Can this be resolved safely?"
       ↓
"If not, which department should handle it?"
       ↓
"Has the department responded?"
       ↓
"Has the issue actually been resolved?"
```

---

# 3. Core philosophy

The entire system follows one principle:

> **AI investigates. Evidence proves. Rules constrain. Risk prioritizes. Authority determines. Humans control consequential decisions. Code executes. Audit remembers.**

This is particularly aligned with Razorpay's published agent principles: verified data, bounded permissions, independent action validation, human approval for sensitive actions, and auditable actions. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

# 16. Module 7 — Decision Engine

Now comes the heart of the system.

The decision engine evaluates:

```text
Evidence
+
Applicable Rules
+
Financial Calculation
+
Risk
+
Decision Authority
+
Agent Permissions
+
Action Safety
```

Then produces one of **four outcomes**.

---

## 🟢 1. AUTO_APPROVE

Conditions:

```text
✓ Required evidence complete
✓ Rules satisfied
✓ Financial calculation verified
✓ Authorized decision owner
✓ Agent has permission
✓ Action within configured limits
✓ No unresolved conflict
✓ No mandatory human/Razorpay review
```

Then:

```text
AUTO_APPROVE
      ↓
EXECUTE
      ↓
VERIFY
      ↓
AUDIT
```

---

## 🔴 2. REJECT

Use this only when the request is **clearly invalid or not permitted** based on applicable rules/policy.

It does **not** mean "something failed."

Example:

```text
Requested action
      ↓
Applicable rule
      ↓
Clear violation
      ↓
REJECT
```

The system must provide the evidence and source supporting the rejection.

---

## 🟠 3. FRAUD_ESCALATE

When evidence indicates possible fraud/fabrication/suspicious activity requiring specialist investigation:

```text
Possible fraud
      ↓
Evidence collection
      ↓
Risk assessment
      ↓
FRAUD_ESCALATE
      ↓
Fraud/Risk Department Ticket
```

The AI should say:

> **"Possible fraudulent activity detected; investigation required."**

not:

> "This is definitely fraud."

That preserves the distinction between an AI-generated risk signal and an authorized final determination.

---

## 🟡 4. DEPARTMENT_ESCALATE

Used when:

```text
Evidence incomplete
OR
Authoritative records conflict
OR
Decision authority is external
OR
Policy is ambiguous
OR
Action exceeds permission
OR
High-risk manual review required
```

Then:

```text
DEPARTMENT_ESCALATE
       ↓
CREATE TICKET
       ↓
ASSIGN DEPARTMENT
       ↓
TRACK
       ↓
REMIND
       ↓
RESOLVE
```

# 37. Final project identity

### **Project Name**
**AI Financial Exception Resolution & Risk Management System**

### **Core Problem**
Financial exceptions are difficult to resolve because transaction information, evidence, policies, risk signals, decisions, and departmental follow-ups are distributed across multiple workflows.

### **Solution**
An AI-powered financial operations system that reconstructs financial events, investigates exceptions using authoritative evidence, applies versioned rules and policies, assesses financial/fraud/operational/compliance risk, and determines whether a case can be automatically resolved, rejected, or escalated.

### **Escalation**
Uncertain or high-risk cases automatically generate structured departmental tickets containing the issue, investigation, evidence, applicable rules, risk factors, decision rationale, and required action. Unresolved tickets are tracked and reminded until resolution.

### **Traceability**
Every decision is linked to:

> **Evidence → Source → Rule → Risk → Authority → Decision → Action → Verification**

### **Final principle**

> **No unsupported decision. No unexplained risk. No untracked escalation. No silent failure.**
