Legal Terms and Privacy Notice

Pre launch legal template. This text must be completed with the company legal name, contact details, retention schedule, deployment locations and counsel approval before any production use.

Terms of Use

These Terms govern access to the Cause AI financial-exception investigation service. The contracting entity is [Company Legal Name], with its registered address at [Registered Address]. The effective date is [Effective Date].

The service helps authorised business users review payment-operation exceptions, assemble evidence and route investigations. It does not execute payments, hold customer funds, replace required human approvals, or make a final fraud determination.

Authorised access

Customers must provide accurate account information, protect credentials, use least-privilege access, promptly disable departing users, and remain responsible for activity under their accounts. Access may be suspended for a security risk, misuse, legal requirement or material breach.

Acceptable use

Users may process only data they are authorised to provide and may not upload payment-card credentials, credentials for third-party services, malware, unlawful content, or personal data beyond the approved purpose. Users may not bypass access controls, probe the service, reverse engineer it where prohibited by law, or use outputs as the sole basis for a high-impact decision about an individual.

Customer responsibility and review

Customers remain responsible for source-data accuracy, their legal basis for processing, customer notices, approvals, reconciliation and final operational decisions. Risk scores and automated routing are decision support; material actions require review under the customer’s approved control framework.

Confidentiality and ownership

Each party must protect the other party’s confidential information with reasonable safeguards and use it only for the service relationship. Customers retain rights in customer data. The provider retains rights in the service, documentation and independently developed improvements.

Service limits

Service levels, support, liability limits, warranties, indemnities, governing law, dispute process and any data-processing agreement must be set out in the signed customer agreement. This public template does not create a payment-service, banking, legal, tax or regulatory advisory relationship.

Privacy Notice

Data fiduciary and contact: [Company Legal Name], [Registered Address], [Privacy Email]. For India, include the Data Protection Officer or grievance contact when required.

Data processed

The service may process business-user account details, contact information, tenant and role identifiers, payment references, amounts, currency, case evidence, audit events, device and security logs, and investigation comments. It must not store card PAN, CVV, or full card-on-file data. Payment data should be minimised to the fields necessary for the stated investigation purpose.

Purposes

Data is processed to authenticate users, investigate exceptions, prevent fraud, maintain security, meet legal obligations, provide support, and maintain audit records. The production privacy notice must state the applicable lawful basis, consent process where consent is relied upon, and any purpose-specific notices.

Sharing and processors

Data may be shared only with authorised personnel, contracted processors under written data-protection obligations, regulators or law-enforcement authorities where legally required, and parties approved by the customer. The production notice must list material processor categories, transfer safeguards and cross-border transfer details where applicable.

Security and retention

The provider uses access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, vulnerability management, backups and incident procedures appropriate to the deployment. Production must publish and enforce a documented retention schedule, deletion workflow, legal-hold process, backup-expiry process and restoration test evidence. Data is retained only for the stated purpose, contractual requirements and applicable law.

Rights and complaints

Individuals may request access, correction, erasure or grievance handling through [Privacy Email] or [Privacy Portal], subject to identity verification and applicable law. The production process must define response times, escalation, recordkeeping and the relevant regulator or grievance channel.

Security incidents

Suspected incidents must be assessed, contained, documented and reported to affected customers and authorities where required by applicable law and contract. The final incident plan must identify responsible contacts, decision authority, notification timelines and evidence preservation steps.

India Payment and Data Requirements

For India deployments, legal and security review must address the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and its phased Rules, RBI requirements relevant to the entity’s role, RBI restrictions on card-on-file storage, payment-system data localisation requirements, CERT-In directions, and any NPCI or UPI obligations applicable to the service. Product claims must match implemented controls and the company’s regulatory status.
