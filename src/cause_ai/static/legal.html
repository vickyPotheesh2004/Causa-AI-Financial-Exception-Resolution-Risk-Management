Causa Demo Terms and Privacy Notice

Synthetic demonstration only. Causa is an independent prototype developed for educational, evaluation and buildathon purposes using fictional financial records and public technical documentation. It is not an official Razorpay product, banking service, payment service or financial institution.

Terms of Use

This page describes the intended use of the Causa demonstration. The application is provided for educational, engineering and evaluation purposes only.

Causa demonstrates financial-exception detection, evidence investigation, risk assessment, policy evaluation, operational routing, verification and audit workflows using synthetic records. It does not execute real payments, refunds or transfers, hold customer funds, access production payment records, or modify production financial accounts.

Authorized Use

Users should access the demonstration only for legitimate development, evaluation, testing and educational purposes. Users must not submit real payment credentials, authentication secrets, confidential financial records or unnecessary personal information.

Acceptable Use

Users must not attempt to bypass authentication or authorization controls, interfere with the service, introduce malicious code, submit payment-card credentials, expose third-party credentials, or use the demonstration to make consequential decisions about real individuals or financial accounts.

AI-Assisted Investigation

Causa may use an AI provider to assist with investigation summaries, evidence interpretation, possible root-cause analysis and recommended investigation steps. AI output is advisory. AI cannot approve payments, authorize refunds, modify financial records, override policy controls, modify audit history or execute financial transactions. Deterministic policy, risk, permission and decision controls remain authoritative.

Synthetic Data and Razorpay Relationship

The demonstration uses fictional financial records. It is not connected to Razorpay production systems and does not process real Razorpay customer or merchant transactions. Public Razorpay documentation may be referenced for contextual design or educational purposes; this does not establish affiliation, endorsement, partnership or authorization by Razorpay.

Service Limitations

This demonstration does not provide contractual service levels, warranties, support commitments, payment-processing services, banking services, legal advice, tax advice or regulatory advice.

Privacy Notice

Demo data notice: this deployment is intended to use synthetic records only. Do not enter real personal information, payment information, authentication credentials or confidential business information.

Data and Purpose

The demonstration may contain synthetic user, merchant, transaction, exception, evidence, risk, ticket, audit and operational records. These records show financial-exception detection, investigation, evidence grounding, risk assessment, deterministic decisioning, operational routing, verification and audit workflows.

AI Provider

Where AI investigation is enabled, Causa may send the minimum necessary synthetic investigation context to the configured OpenRouter model for advisory analysis. Users must not provide real personal, payment or confidential production information.

Security

The application implements demonstration-level security controls including authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, security headers, input validation, request controls and audit logging. A production deployment would additionally require independently managed secrets, durable storage, encryption and key management, backups, monitoring, vulnerability management, incident response, disaster recovery and formal security review.

Retention and Privacy Requests

This demonstration is not intended to provide production data retention, archival or privacy-rights services. A future production deployment would require a documented retention schedule, deletion workflow, backup-expiry process, legal-hold process, restoration testing, legal entity, privacy contact and applicable grievance mechanism.

Security Incidents

Any future production deployment would require a documented incident-response process covering detection, containment, investigation, evidence preservation, notification and recovery in accordance with applicable law and contractual obligations.
