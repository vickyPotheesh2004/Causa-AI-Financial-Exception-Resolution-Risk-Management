[Normal] # **AI Financial Exception Resolution & Risk Management System**
[Normal] ### One-line definition
[Normal] ---
[Normal] # 1. The Problem
[Normal] Financial operations can involve multiple connected records:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Payment
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Order
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Refund
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Fees
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Adjustments
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Settlement
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Bank Record
[Normal] ```
[Normal] An exception can occur anywhere in this lifecycle.
[Normal] For example:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Payment = ₹10,000
[Normal] Expected Settlement = ₹9,750
[Normal] Actual Settlement = ₹9,500
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] ₹250 mismatch
[Normal] ```
[Normal] A finance employee then has to manually:
[Normal] - find the transaction,
[Normal] - find the settlement,
[Normal] - inspect fees,
[Normal] - inspect refunds,
[Normal] - compare bank records,
[Normal] - determine what happened,
[Normal] - check applicable rules,
[Normal] - determine whether the issue is valid,
[Normal] - assess financial/fraud/compliance risk,
[Normal] - decide whether it can be resolved,
[Normal] - contact the appropriate department,
[Normal] - track the ticket,
[Normal] - remember to follow up,
[Normal] - and finally document everything.
[Normal] The problem is therefore **not simply reconciliation**.
[Normal] The deeper problem is:
[Normal] ---
[Normal] # 2. What the system actually solves
[Normal] The system converts:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] "Something doesn't match."
[Normal] ```
[Normal] into:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] "What happened?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "What evidence proves it?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "Which rules apply?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "Who has authority to decide?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "What is the risk?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "Can this be resolved safely?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "If not, which department should handle it?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "Has the department responded?"
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] "Has the issue actually been resolved?"
[Normal] ```
[Normal] ---
[Normal] # 3. Core philosophy
[Normal] The entire system follows one principle:
[Normal] > **AI investigates. Evidence proves. Rules constrain. Risk prioritizes. Authority determines. Humans control consequential decisions. Code executes. Audit remembers.**

[Normal] ---
[Normal] ## 🟢 1. AUTO_APPROVE
[Normal] Conditions:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] ✓ Required evidence complete
[Normal] ✓ Rules satisfied
[Normal] ✓ Financial calculation verified
[Normal] ✓ Authorized decision owner
[Normal] ✓ Agent has permission
[Normal] ✓ Action within configured limits
[Normal] ✓ No unresolved conflict
[Normal] ✓ No mandatory human/Razorpay review
[Normal] ```
[Normal] Then:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] AUTO_APPROVE
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] EXECUTE
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] VERIFY
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] AUDIT
[Normal] ```
[Normal] ---
[Normal] ## 🔴 2. REJECT
[Normal] Use this only when the request is **clearly invalid or not permitted** based on applicable rules/policy.
[Normal] It does **not** mean "something failed."
[Normal] Example:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Requested action
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Applicable rule
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Clear violation
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] REJECT
[Normal] ```
[Normal] The system must provide the evidence and source supporting the rejection.
[Normal] ---
[Normal] ## 🟠 3. FRAUD_ESCALATE
[Normal] When evidence indicates possible fraud/fabrication/suspicious activity requiring specialist investigation:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Possible fraud
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Evidence collection
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Risk assessment
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] FRAUD_ESCALATE
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] Fraud/Risk Department Ticket
[Normal] ```
[Normal] The AI should say:
[Normal] > **"Possible fraudulent activity detected; investigation required."**
[Normal] not:
[Normal] > "This is definitely fraud."
[Normal] That preserves the distinction between an AI-generated risk signal and an authorized final determination.
[Normal] ---
[Normal] ## 🟡 4. DEPARTMENT_ESCALATE
[Normal] Used when:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Evidence incomplete
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Authoritative records conflict
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Decision authority is external
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Policy is ambiguous
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Action exceeds permission
[Normal] OR
[Normal] High-risk manual review required
[Normal] ```
[Normal] Then:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] DEPARTMENT_ESCALATE
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] CREATE TICKET
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] ASSIGN DEPARTMENT
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] TRACK
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] REMIND
[Normal] ↓
[Normal] RESOLVE
[Normal] ```

[Normal] # 16. Decision Engine
[Normal] This should be **deterministic**, not LLM-controlled.
[Normal] ```text

[Normal] +

[Normal] Risk
[Normal] +

[Normal] +

[Normal] │
[Normal] ┌────┼─────────────┬────────────────┐
[Normal] ▼    ▼             ▼                ▼

[Normal] ### AUTO_APPROVE
[Normal] Only if:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Evidence sufficient
[Normal] AND
[Normal] Rules pass
[Normal] AND
[Normal] Authority known
[Normal] AND
[Normal] Permission exists
[Normal] AND
[Normal] Risk within threshold
[Normal] AND
[Normal] No conflict
[Normal] AND
[Normal] Action is allowed
[Normal] ```
[Normal] ### REJECT
[Normal] Only when the request is clearly invalid/prohibited.
[Normal] ### FRAUD_ESCALATE
[Normal] When evidence indicates possible fraud/suspicious activity.
[Normal] ### DEPARTMENT_ESCALATE
[Normal] When:
[Normal] ```text
[Normal] Evidence insufficient
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Evidence conflicting
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Authority unclear
[Normal] OR
[Normal] High-risk action
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Manual review required
[Normal] OR
[Normal] Policy ambiguous
[Normal] ```
