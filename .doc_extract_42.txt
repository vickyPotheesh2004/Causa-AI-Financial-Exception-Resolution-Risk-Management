Problem Statement When financial records do not agree, there is no unified, evidence-driven workflow that can investigate the exception, determine what is actually known, assess its risk, apply the appropriate rules and authority boundaries, resolve safe cases automatically, escalate uncertain or fraud-related cases, and track the issue until closure.
[TABLE 6]
Product Definition An evidence-driven AI financial operations system that detects, investigates, evaluates, risk-assesses, resolves, rejects, or escalates financial exceptions across payment, settlement, refund, fee, adjustment, and bank records — while enforcing applicable rules and permissions, creating department tickets and reminders for unresolved cases, and maintaining a complete, searchable, source-backed audit trail from detection to final resolution.

The System's Fundamental Contract The system must never make a financial decision merely because the AI thinks it is correct. It must establish the relevant evidence, identify the applicable rule, determine the decision authority, assess risk, verify that the requested action is permitted, and then either execute within its bounded authority, reject a clearly invalid request, or escalate the case to the appropriate department. Every decision, ticket, reminder, action, and resolution must remain traceable to its evidence and source.
